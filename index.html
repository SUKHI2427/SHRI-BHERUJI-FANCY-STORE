Frequently Asked Questions

What are the opening and closing hours of Shri Bheruji Fancy Store?
We are open 7 days a week, from 8:00 AM in the morning to 9:30 PM at night. Whether you need morning pooja items or late-evening emergency school stationery, our doors are open!
Can I bring my saree or blouse piece to match bangles?
Yes, absolutely! In fact, most brides and festive shoppers bring their sarees or photos. Our expert team will sit with you to assemble the exact matching glass, velvet, and metallic kadas.
Do you offer discounts for bulk birthday return gifts?
Yes, we offer special wholesale pricing for orders above 25 pieces for birthdays, navratri, varalakshmi pooja, and corporate gifting. We also offer gift wrapping options!
Are the cosmetic brands in your store 100% genuine?
Yes, 100%. All cosmetics (Lakmé, Maybelline, Colorbar, etc.) are sourced through authorized distributors with sealed packages and verified expiry batch codes.
